## What the search evidence actually established, and what it did not

**Established.** That each of these markets has priced vendors, published rate cards, live job
boards or live competitor listings. That is evidence a market clears — competitor presence is a
purchase signal, and it is the signal available under this session's constraints.

**Not established.** Individually named buyers with a stated, dated, unmet need. The spec asks for
"looking for / need help / can't find" primary posts on Reddit, Upwork job feeds and forums.
Those hosts are all egress-blocked here (`reddit.com`, `upwork.com` job search, `remotive.com`,
`remoteok.com` all return gateway 403). Search returned SEO content marketing *about* those
markets instead of the posts themselves. This is the single largest quality gap in Phase 1 and it
propagates directly into Phase 5 — see `STATE.md`.

The three exceptions, where search surfaced a primary or regulator source rather than a vendor blog:
`findajob.dwp.gov.uk` (official UK government job board, 4 individual listings),
`gov.uk` accessibility monitoring publication, and `ico.org.uk` B2B marketing guidance.

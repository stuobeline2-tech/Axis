## Rule applied
Where automation or AI assistance is prohibited, the platform is **out of scope permanently** —
not "used carefully". No multi-accounting, no impersonation, no evasion of detection. Where AI
assistance is permitted subject to disclosure, we disclose.

## Consequence for Engine B (§8)
The highest-volume AI-suitable task category (annotation) is closed by its own terms. What remains
(transcription at ~$7–20/hour; marketplaces gated on identity verification) sits at or below the
structural prior the spec itself states — platform task work rarely clears £15–25/hour after
screening and compliance. **Engine B is therefore not run as a cash engine in this session.** It
is retained only for its §8.1 function: if a task type recurs and is permissibly automatable, it
is market intelligence pointing at a productisable process. No Engine B account is created —
account creation is a §1.1 Connect gate in any event.

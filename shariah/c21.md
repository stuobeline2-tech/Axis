# Shariah analysis — c21 Transcription platforms (Rev, Verbit, 3Play)
**Verdict: PASSES Shariah at 9/10; rejected on economics.** Defined work, defined per-minute pay,
no riba, no maysir. Rights and confidentiality of client audio must be respected. The reason it
loses is the objective function, not the screen: retrieved data puts a beginner at ~$7/hour and an
expert at ~$20/hour, and the average Rev freelancer at $245/month. Where AI assistance is
permitted it must be disclosed; where it is not, the work must be personally performed, which
consumes exactly the human hours the objective function is trying to conserve.

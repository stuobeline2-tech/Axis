# Shariah analysis — c11 Autonomous SEO content engine sold as a service
**Verdict: EXCLUDED. Certainty 7/10 — below the 8/10 gate.**
1. Sold: automatically generated, automatically published articles on the client's site under the
client's name. Flat monthly fee. 2. **Gharar:** curable (volume, length, topics).
3. **Riba / 4. Maysir:** none.
5. **Rights:** the engine writes new text, but SEO content is assembled from sources; without a
citation discipline the risk of reproducing others' work is real and unmanaged at volume.
6. **Are all claims literally true? — the failing question.** At the volumes that make this
economic (dozens of articles a month, published without human reading), we would be publishing
factual assertions under a client's name that **no one has verified**. That is not a controllable
risk at that price point; controlling it means human review, which destroys the economics that
make the candidate attractive. Publishing unverified claims as a client's own expertise is a
species of deception even when no individual sentence is intended to mislead.
7. **Harm surface:** readers acting on unverified content; the client's own reputation.
8. **Customer core revenue:** varies.
**Decision:** excluded on question 6. Note this does **not** exclude the operator's own use of the
engine on their own site, where the operator reads and owns what is published — it excludes
*selling* unread output as someone else's expertise.

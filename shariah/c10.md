# Shariah analysis — c10 Sacred Resins — frankincense and myrrh export
**Verdict: PASSES. Certainty 9/10.**
1. Sold: a physical commodity, priced per kilogram by grade. 2. **Gharar:** manageable and the
usual trade risk — must specify grade, moisture, origin, quantity, delivery terms and inspection
rights; sale of a specified existing lot is clean, forward sale of an unharvested crop needs care
(*salam* conditions: price paid in full at contract, goods precisely specified, delivery date
fixed). The "2026 harvest pre-order" framing on the live site should be checked against those
conditions. 3. **Riba:** none in the sale itself; avoid trade finance, letters of credit bearing
interest, and factoring. 4. **Maysir:** none. 5. **Rights:** goods lawfully purchased from
harvesters; export/import documentation genuine. 6. **Claims:** lab documentation must be real
(the operator maintains a lab documentation stack — good), no unverified therapeutic claims, which
are also a regulatory offence. 7. **Harm surface:** supply chain — harvester payment terms and
tree over-tapping sustainability. 8. **Customer core revenue:** wholesale/retail buyers — screen
for incense sold into practices we would not wish to supply.

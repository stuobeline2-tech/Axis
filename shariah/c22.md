# Shariah analysis — c22 Open freelance marketplaces (Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer)
**Verdict: PASSES Shariah at 8/10; blocked at the Connect gate and weak on economics.**
Defined work for defined pay; no riba or maysir. Two conditions: (i) Upwork's 2026 rules permit AI
*assistance* but require the freelancer to personally review and customise all client
communications and prohibit fully automated submission — so any use here is human-in-the-loop by
rule, and misrepresenting authorship would be deception; (ii) account creation and identity
verification is a §1.1 **Connect** gate and cannot be done autonomously. Note also the 5 Jan 2026
User Agreement change under which content created on the platform may train Upwork's models — a
consideration for any client-confidential work.

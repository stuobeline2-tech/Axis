# Shariah analysis — c06 SC-cleared data analyst contracting
**Verdict: PASSES. Certainty 9/10.**
1. Sold: the operator's own labour by the day. Consideration: a day rate. Classic *ijārah*.
2. **Gharar:** low — day rate, stated hours, stated scope. 3. **Riba:** none, unless the assignment
itself is at a bank or lender working on interest-bearing products, which must be declined.
4. **Maysir:** none. 5. **Rights:** employer's data under employment/contract terms; SC clearance
obligations bind absolutely. 6. **Claims:** CV must be literally true. 7. **Harm:** low.
8. **Client core revenue:** government/public sector generally fine; decline placements at
conventional banks, insurers, betting operators and alcohol businesses.
**Note:** this is selling hours, not building an engine. It passes the Shariah gate cleanly and
scores 5.45 — its weakness is structural (no automation, no repeatability), not moral.

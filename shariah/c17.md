# Shariah analysis — c17 Ecommerce product data cleanup / enrichment
**Verdict: PASSES. Certainty 9/10.** Flat or per-SKU fee for defined attribute standardisation and
deduplication. **Gharar** removed by fixing SKU count and rule set. **Riba/maysir:** none.
**Rights:** product data supplied by the client; generated descriptions must not be lifted from
competitors' copy. **Claims:** enriched attributes must be accurate — inventing product attributes
to fill a field is fabrication and directly misleads consumers, so unknown attributes are left
empty and reported, never guessed. **Customer core revenue:** screen the catalogue; decline
alcohol, adult, gambling and counterfeit-risk marketplaces.

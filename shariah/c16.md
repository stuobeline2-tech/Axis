# Shariah analysis — c16 Google Business Profile audit / local SEO
**Verdict: PASSES. Certainty 8/10.**
1. Sold: a profile audit and optimisation. Flat setup fee or monthly management.
2-4. **Gharar** curable; **riba/maysir** none. 5. **Rights:** the client's own profile, accessed
with their authority; platform terms respected. 6. **Claims:** no ranking guarantees.
7. **Harm surface — the decisive condition:** this industry's standard growth tactics include
**soliciting or fabricating reviews**, keyword-stuffing the business name, and creating fake
location listings. Fabricated reviews are excluded outright by §2.2 and Google's own terms.
Passing requires that the service is limited to genuine profile completeness, accurate categories,
real photos, real hours, and answering real questions. That excludes a large share of what
competitors actually sell. 8. **Customer core revenue:** screen individually.

# Shariah analysis — c08 UK public sector WCAG 2.2 accessibility audit (RUNNER-UP)
**Verdict: PASSES. Certainty 9/10** — the cleanest candidate in the set.

## 1. What is sold / consideration
Sold: a written accessibility audit of a public sector website against WCAG 2.2 AA — enumerated
failures, each mapped to the specific success criterion, with the remediation required and a
prioritisation by user impact. Consideration: a flat fee in money.

## 2. Gharar
Low and easily removed: scope is a stated list of page templates and user journeys, tested to a
stated standard, delivered in a stated format by a stated date. Nothing depends on a third party's
discretion.

## 3. Riba
None. Flat fee, no financing, no credit, no interest-bearing element anywhere in the chain. Public
bodies pay from budget by BACS.

## 4. Maysir
None.

## 5. Rights to assets
Testing a public website against a published open standard (W3C WCAG 2.2). Open-source testing
engines (axe-core, pa11y) are permissively licensed — licence terms must be checked and honoured
before any is bundled. No scraping beyond fetching pages the body publishes for public reading,
rate-limited, robots.txt respected.

## 6. Claims true
Every finding is reproducible by the client on their own site. This is the candidate where truth is
easiest to guarantee, because the evidence ships with the deliverable.

## 7. Harm surface
Strongly **positive**. The direct beneficiaries are disabled users of public services. The only
harm risk is selling an audit to a body that does not need one, or overstating severity to
manufacture urgency — both refused; findings are reported at their actual WCAG conformance level.

## 8. Customer's core revenue
Public bodies funded by taxation. Permissible. (Individual exclusions if a specific body's function
were impermissible — none expected in scope: councils, NHS trusts, schools, agencies.)

## Why it is the runner-up rather than the winner
See `DECISIONS.md`. Nothing wrong with it; it loses on **cash speed**. Public procurement cannot
close same-day, which the objective function weights at 0.20 and where this scores 1/10.

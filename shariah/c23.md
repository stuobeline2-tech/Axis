# Shariah analysis — c23 B2B lead list building / enrichment as a service
**Verdict: KILLED. Certainty 6/10.**

## 1-4
Sold: compiled contact records. Flat or per-record fee — no riba, no maysir, gharar curable.

## 5. Rights to assets — the killing objection
The product **is** other people's personal data. We would be collecting identifiable individuals'
work contact details and selling them onward to buyers we do not control, for purposes those
individuals have not been told about and cannot inspect. Even where PECR permits *us* to email a
corporate subscriber (`ico.org.uk` guidance, retrieved), that permission is about our own marketing;
it is not a right to commercialise the data as a product. Under UK GDPR, transparency (Art. 14)
and purpose limitation would need to hold across every downstream buyer, and they cannot, because
we do not control the downstream use.

## 7. Harm surface
The harm lands on people who are not our customers and never agreed to anything, and it compounds
with every resale. Building prospect data **for our own outreach**, with our own LIA and our own
suppression list, remains permissible and is exactly what Phase 5 does. Selling that data is
a different transaction, and it is the one killed here.

## 8
Buyers' core revenue varies and would be uncontrollable.

## Decision
Killed on questions 5 and 7. Note the distinction preserved: *use* for our own compliant outreach,
yes; *sale* as a product, no.

# Shariah analysis — c02 Offshore virtual medical admin / biller placement
**Verdict: PASSES. Certainty 8/10.**
1. **Sold/consideration:** recruitment, vetting and ongoing management of a remote administrative
   worker placed with a US practice. Consideration: a monthly fee in money.
2. **Gharar:** curable — role, hours, coverage window, replacement terms and notice must be
   written. Left vague, "a VA" is under-specified; the offer must enumerate duties.
3. **Riba:** none in the fee. Watch one leg: we must not withhold worker pay pending client
   payment and then charge for the float, and must not pay workers via any advance/credit product.
4. **Maysir:** none.
5. **Rights:** worker's consent to placement, client's data under processing instruction.
6. **Claims true:** no inflated claims about staff qualifications or HIPAA training that has not
   actually been delivered.
7. **Harm surface:** the material one is **to the placed worker** — this is a labour arbitrage.
   Requires: wages paid in full and on time, stated in the contract, no unpaid trial periods, no
   passport/document retention, no penalty deductions. A placement business that squeezes the
   worker to widen margin fails this screen even though the customer is happy.
8. **Customer core revenue:** medical practice — permissible, same exclusion list as c01.

# Shariah analysis — c03 Unsold-estimate reactivation, US home service contractors
**Verdict: PASSES. Certainty 8/10.**
1. **Sold/consideration:** follow-up campaigns to a contractor's own prior estimate recipients,
   under the contractor's brand. Flat pilot fee, then flat monthly retainer.
2. **Gharar:** curable and already handled in the pre-existing repo — fixed fee, fixed duration,
   fixed touch count. No percentage of recovered jobs (which would be both gharar and a share in
   proceeds).
3. **Riba:** the live risk in this trade is **contractor financing**, which is interest-based and
   is the standard close mechanic in home services. The pre-existing README already commits to
   neither building nor promoting it; that commitment is what keeps this candidate alive.
4. **Maysir:** none under flat fee.
5. **Rights:** contact only the *client's own* prior estimate contacts, with the client's written
   authority — never a scraped or purchased list. Already the stated rule in `delivery/`.
6. **Claims true:** no borrowed case studies presented as our results; the call script already
   opens by naming itself a cold call.
7. **Harm surface:** the homeowner receiving follow-up. Bounded by: existing relationship, client
   brand, opt-out honoured, no manufactured urgency or fake expiring discounts.
8. **Customer core revenue:** roofing/HVAC/plumbing — permissible.

# Shariah analysis — c14 UK bid / tender writing for SMEs
**Verdict: EXCLUDED. Certainty 7/10 — below the 8/10 gate.**
1. Sold: written tender responses. Flat fee per bid. 2-4. **Gharar/riba/maysir:** curable / none.
5. **Rights:** the client's material.
6. **Are all claims literally true? — the failing question.** A bid writer's product is a set of
assertions about *the client's* capability, experience, staffing and track record, submitted to a
public body which will rely on them and award public money accordingly. We cannot verify most of
them, and the commercial pressure in this trade runs entirely towards presenting the client more
favourably than the evidence supports. Signing our work to statements we cannot verify, in a
document that induces a public authority to contract, is a misrepresentation risk we would carry
on every engagement. 7. **Harm surface:** the awarding authority and the competing bidders who
lose to an overstated bid. 8. **Customer core revenue:** varies.
**Decision:** excluded on question 6. A narrower variant — editing and structuring text the client
has themselves asserted and evidenced, with no capability claims authored by us — could be
re-screened later, but it is not what the market pays £1,500–£7,000 for.

# Shariah analysis — c12 Bookkeeping catch-up / QuickBooks cleanup
**Verdict: PASSES. Certainty 8/10.**
1. Sold: reconciled books to a stated date. Flat fee, priced by months behind and transaction
volume. 2. **Gharar:** curable — quote after a scoped diagnostic, not before. 3. **Riba:** none in
the fee. The books being reconciled will contain the client's own interest income and loan
interest; recording an existing transaction accurately is not participating in it. Decline
engagements where the work is *arranging* interest-bearing finance. 4. **Maysir:** none.
5. **Rights:** client's financial records under processor terms. 6. **Claims:** no implication of
being a licensed accountant or tax agent if we are not — this is a regulated-adjacent area and
misrepresentation here is both haram and unlawful. 7. **Harm surface:** genuinely high — errors
propagate into tax filings. Requires competence we must honestly hold, plus PII cover.
8. **Customer core revenue:** screen individually.

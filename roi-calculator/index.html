Assumptions & limits — read this part

  • The only figure not supplied by you is the recovery rate, shown above and adjustable.
  • This is an estimate, not a guarantee. Actual results depend on your list quality, how old the estimates are, your pricing, and whether the work is still needed.
  • Revenue shown is top-line, not profit. Your own material and labour costs come out of it.
  • We count a job as recovered only when you confirm it closed — logged and reviewed with you.
  • Follow-up goes to your existing estimate contacts, under your brand, with your approval. We do not cold-contact strangers on your behalf.
Fee is flat and fixed in advance — no percentage of revenue, no interest, no charges contingent on financing products. If a pilot does not produce, we report that plainly.
